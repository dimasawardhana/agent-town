# Building roofs

Give a building's top a shape chosen by hashing its directory path, so a district
stops being a row of identical boxes.

A roof is deliberately **ornament and nothing else**. It carries no reading about
the code: a building's height still means source bytes, its footprint still means
source-file count, and its ladder rank and damage still mean work and failure. The
roof is the one channel that means nothing, which is why it is confined to the cap
where it cannot be mistaken for a claim.

The design and the reasoning — including the alternatives rejected (role-reading
variants, a new Place for "airport/station", the metaphor-intensity setting) and
the measured cel budget — live in `docs/adr/0021-roofs-are-ornament-confined-to-the-cap.md`.
The glossary term is **Roof** in `CONTEXT.md`.

This directory holds the implementation tickets. One ticket per file, numbered in
dependency order.
