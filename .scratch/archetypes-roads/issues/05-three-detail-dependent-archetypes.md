# 05 — Three archetypes that lean on detail, and may be cut

**What to build:** A Restaurant, a Market and a School join the vocabulary —
each identified by surface detail rather than by outline.

These three are drawn last, and deliberately. A Restaurant is a pitched roof
that must be told from a Cottage by its awning; a Market is a stall row, which
is texture rather than silhouette; a School is a tall pitched roof that must be
told from a Restaurant. All three depend on detail surviving at 91px, which is
the one thing this design cannot assume.

If one of them does not read, **cut it** rather than enlarge it. The vocabulary
stands at nine without any of the three, and the atlas has room for all twelve
— but legibility is worth more than count, and 64 cels of headroom is worth more
than three names.

## Comments

This is the only ticket in the set with an explicitly acceptable outcome of
*shipping fewer things than it set out to ship*. That is the point of sequencing
the detail-dependent archetypes last: cutting one now costs a drawing, and
cutting one after the vocabulary was presented as twelve would cost a
conversation.

If all three survive, the axis reaches twelve and the atlas holds 936 cels of
1408. If one is cut, it holds 904 and the spare grows.
